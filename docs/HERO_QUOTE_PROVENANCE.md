## Literal Latin (exact homepage H1)

> Quam multa fieri non posse prius quam sunt facta iudicantur?

**Author:** Gaius Plinius Secundus (Pliny the Elder, Плиний Старший).
**Work:** *Naturalis Historia*, Book VII, **section 6** (within the opening chapter).
**Century:** first century CE (I век н. э.); the year in which this particular sentence was written is not known.
**Russian editorial translation (small line under H1):**

> Как много вещей считают невозможными, пока они не осуществлены?

This Russian rendering is ours for the site, **not** a quotation from an identified historical Russian translator. The meaning is consistent with published English translations.

The immediate context is a warning against treating unfamiliar or unobserved things as incredible or impossible. **It does not claim every mathematically impossible outcome is achievable.** A scientific institution should not suggest that the line is a formal universal claim.
